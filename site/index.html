Hand your agent a document, PDF, HTML form, or photo of a paper form. It installs Nimmari, prepares and prefills the document, confirms signers, and delivers signing links in chat. You sign and fill only what your agent cannot know, such as a personal ID.

How it works

Your agent does the work.
You hand over the document and sign.

  1. Install and configure. Give your agent Render and Turso provider keys privately. It deploys and completes POST /api/setup/claim, storing separate admin and document keys.
  2. Hand over the material. Your agent crops and straightens a photo into a PDF locally, inspects your PDF, or builds a self-contained HTML form. It confirms who signs, how many signers there are, which line belongs to each, and which blanks to fill.
  3. Prepare and prefill. It uploads via POST /api/documents (PDF) or POST /api/documents/html. PDF fields use PUT /api/documents/{id}/fields; HTML fields use data-pm-field. It assigns signer keys, fetches fields with GET /api/documents/{id}, prefills authorized known values with PUT /api/documents/{id}/prefill, and checks placement visually. Signatures stay yours.
  4. Get your link in chat. Your agent sends confirmed signer slots with POST /api/documents/{id}/send and notify:false, verifies emailSent:false, and privately delivers each returned URL to its intended signer. Upload multipart notify="false" also suppresses completion emails. No email delivery or signer account is needed; signer email is still required for the audit trail. Links expire after 30 days.
  5. Sign, then collect. You open your link, fill remaining unknown fields, and sign. Your agent polls GET /api/documents/{id} or reconciles lifecycle webhooks set by upload webhookUrl. After completed, it downloads and validates GET /api/documents/{id}/pdf?signed=1. It stops on cancellation or decline.

For sequential signing without email, your agent delivers later links when earlier slots finish. Webhooks cover completion, cancellation, and decline; verify raw-body HMAC with the server’s webhook secret and keep polling as a fallback because delivery is best effort.

Use the portable agent skill → Its helpers prepare drafts, check prefill, and poll for a validated signed PDF. The owner web UI and manual setup remain secondary alternatives.

From document to signing link

A link for the signature.

PDFs & HTML forms

Place text, multiline text, checkbox, date, personal identity text, and signature fields. Import existing PDF AcroForm fields or use HTML form mode.

One signer or several

Use parallel or sequential signer slots, including distinct roles with the same email. Signers need no account; signing links expire after 30 days.

Prepare, then send

Agents prefill known values; only signatures are required by default. Use private signing views when needed. Documents support cancellation and signer decline.

Audit seals

Combined audit seals record signing metadata, with timezone controls and a SHA-256 content hash.

Connect your workflow

Use scoped, hashed API keys and admin account provisioning. Optional Resend email and outbound lifecycle webhooks connect your instance to other services.

Choose your infrastructure

Run the Docker image on Render or your own host. Documents and signed outputs live in your configured database; back it up and test restores.

Run your own Nimmari

Let your agent set it up.

The default fresh hosted setup uses a Render API key and a Turso Platform API token. Your agent deploys the instance, then completes the one-time setup claim.

The claim creates your first account and separate admin and regular agent keys. No server root key is needed. Provide credentials privately through your agent’s secret manager or environment, and keep them outside the repository.

Know what you’re signing

Simple Electronic Signatures.

Nimmari produces Simple Electronic Signatures (SES) under eIDAS. These are not Advanced or Qualified Electronic Signatures. PAdES and trusted timestamps are not included.

You are responsible for deciding whether SES is sufficient for your use case, jurisdiction, and document type. This is not legal advice. Nimmari is provided without warranty under AGPL-3.0.

Private fields are hidden from other signers during signing; completed PDFs expose all values to all parties. Optional email and remote databases use external providers. You are the data controller for your instance.
